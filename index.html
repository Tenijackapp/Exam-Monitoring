Import questions from text

Paste your questions in either style: the tagged format below, or straight out of a real exam document — numbered questions ("1. ..."), lettered options ("A." or "A)"), and section headings like "Part I. Multiple Choice" all get recognized. "(3 pts)" after a question sets its point value automatically.

MC: What is the capital of France? A) Paris * B) London C) Berlin TF: The mitochondria is the powerhouse of the cell. = True ID: What powers a cell? = mitochondria = mitochondrion ENUM: Name the parts of a cell. = Nucleus = Mitochondria = Ribosome ESSAY: Explain the water cycle.

Mark the correct MC option with a trailing *. For true/false, put = True or = False on its own line. List each accepted identification answer on its own line starting with =. For enumeration, list each expected item on its own line starting with = — add a parenthetical or a " / " to give an item an accepted synonym, e.g. = Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU). Separate questions with a blank line.

Before you begin

This exam is monitored. Switching tabs or minimizing this window is logged with a timestamp. Your camera will take periodic snapshots (not continuous video) to check for phone use, and one snapshot is taken any time you switch away. A visible camera indicator stays on the whole time.
